Tribute to Jimmy Buffett

James Buffett, singer-songwriter, musician, author & businessman was born in 1946 and was best known for his hit song Margaritaville. He was also able to turn the popularity of his song and career into a successful brand, licensing hotels, bars, restaurants, casinos, and cruises. He promoted enjoying life and following ones passions.

Achievements

  1. 20 million certified albums sold worldwide
  2. 27 studio albums and more than 40 tours
  3. 23 licsensed 'Jimmy Buffett's Margaritaville' restuarants and hotels
  4. Posthumorsly inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame
  5. Best Selling Author
